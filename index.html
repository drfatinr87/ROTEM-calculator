Cleaned-Up ROTEM Interpretation Flowchart

Top-down sequential decision pathway for active diffuse or massive bleeding (Trauma vs. Obstetric PPH).

STEP 1: STOP FIBRINOLYSIS First Priority

Is EXTEM A5 < 35 mm OR FIBTEM CT > 600 s (flat-line) OR ML ≥ 5% (Trauma) / ≥ 10% (PPH)?

YES → Give Tranexamic Acid (TXA): Trauma: 25 mg/kg IV single bolus (<3h) • PPH: 1–2 g IV single bolus.

↓
STEP 2: BUILD CLOT FIRMNESS Trigger: EXTEM A5 < 35 mm (or low A5_FIB / platelet dysfunction)
SEVERE DEFICIT (EXTEM A5 < 15 mm [Trauma] / ≤ 15 mm [PPH]): Give 2 Platelet Concentrates + Fibrinogen (≥ 4 g) immediately!
LOW FIBRINOGEN

FIBTEM A5 < 9 mm (Trauma) | < 10.4 mm (PPH)

  • Target A5_FIB: ≥ 12 mm (Trauma) | ≥ 13.9 mm (PPH)
  • Dose (g) = [Target rise × Wt (kg)] / 160
  • 1 U Cryo ≈ 300 mg Fib (10 U Cryo ≈ 2 g)
LOW PLATELETS / DYSFUNCTION

FIBTEM A5 ≥ 9 mm (Trauma) | ≥ 10.4 mm (PPH)

  • A5_EX < 35 mm (or ADPTEM <40 Trauma / <35 PPH): 1 pooled/apheresis PC (or 1U/15kg; Paeds 15 mL/kg)
  • A5_EX < 25 mm (or low ADPTEM + TRAPTEM): 2 pooled/apheresis PCs
  • DAPT or ADPTEM < 30: Consider DDAVP 0.3 µg/kg + TXA
↓
STEP 3: FIX CLOT INITIATION (CT) After A5_FIB is corrected

EXTEM CT > 80 s (with adequate A5_FIB)

Extrinsic Factor Deficiency: Give 4F-PCC (Trauma: 15–25 IU/kg | PPH: 10–15 IU/kg) OR FFP (10–15 mL/kg). If FFP fails & A5 OK: consider rFVIIa 45–90 µg/kg.

INTEM CT > 240 s → Compare HEPTEM CT

• HEPTEM normalizes (CT_IN/CT_HEP ≥ 1.25): Protamine 25–50 mg.
• HEPTEM still > 240 s: FFP 10–15 mL/kg (or rFVIIa 90 µg/kg in PPH acquired hemophilia).

↓
STEP 4: RE-EVALUATE

Ongoing bleeding? → Repeat ROTEM 10–15 minutes after transfusion using a new 3 mL citrate sample.
