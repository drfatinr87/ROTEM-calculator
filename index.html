Cleaned-Up ROTEM Interpretation Flowchart

Top-down sequential decision pathway for active diffuse or massive bleeding.

STEP 1: STOP FIBRINOLYSIS First Priority

Is EXTEM A5 < 35 mm  OR  FIBTEM CT > 600 s (flat-line)  OR  ML ≥ 5% (Trauma) / ≥ 10% (PPH)?

YES → Give Tranexamic Acid (TXA):
  • Trauma: 25 mg/kg IV single bolus (within 3 hours)
  • PPH: 1–2 g IV single bolus
↓
STEP 2: BUILD CLOT FIRMNESS Trigger: EXTEM A5 < 35 mm
SEVERE DEFICIT (EXTEM A5 < 20 mm): Give BOTH Cryoprecipitate/Fibrinogen AND Platelets immediately!
Low Fibrinogen

FIBTEM A5 < 9 mm (Trauma)
FIBTEM A5 < 10.4 mm (PPH)

Action: Cryoprecipitate OR Fibrinogen Concentrate

  • Target A5FIB: ≥ 12 mm (Trauma) | ≥ 13.9 mm (PPH)
  • Dose (g): [Target rise × Wt (kg)] / 160
  • 1 U Cryo ≈ 300 mg Fib (10 U ≈ 2 g)
Low Platelets

FIBTEM A5 ≥ 9 mm (Trauma)
FIBTEM A5 ≥ 10.4 mm (PPH)

Action: Transfuse Platelet Concentrate

  • Adult: 1 unit per 15 kg (4–6 random units) or 1 apheresis pack
  • Paeds: 15 mL/kg BW
↓
STEP 3: FIX CLOT INITIATION (CT) After A5FIB is corrected

EXTEM CT > 80 s (with adequate A5FIB)

Extrinsic Factor Deficiency:
Give 4F-PCC (15–25 IU/kg) OR FFP (10–15 mL/kg).

INTEM CT > 240 s → Compare HEPTEM CT

• HEPTEM normalizes (CTIN/CTHEP ≥ 1.25): Heparin effect → Protamine 25–50 mg
• HEPTEM still > 240 s: Intrinsic deficiency → FFP 10–15 mL/kg

↓
STEP 4: RE-EVALUATE

Ongoing bleeding? → Repeat ROTEM 10–15 minutes after transfusion using a new 3 mL citrate sample.
