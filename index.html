3. Guided Transfusion & Dosing Plan

Cleaned-Up ROTEM A5 Algorithm

Step 1: Stop Fibrinolysis

Trigger: A5_EX < 35 mm OR CT_FIB > 600 s OR ML ≥ 5–10%

TXA: Trauma 25 mg/kg IV bolus (<3h) | PPH 1–2 g IV bolus.

Step 2: Build Clot Firmness (EXTEM A5 < 35 mm)
Severe (< 20 mm)

Give BOTH Cryo/Fibrinogen + Platelets immediately.

Low Fibrinogen

A5_FIB < 9 mm (Trauma) or < 10.4 mm (PPH).
Target: ≥ 12 mm (Trauma) | ≥ 13.9 mm (PPH).

Low Platelets

A5_FIB ≥ 9 mm (Trauma) or > 10.4 mm (PPH).
Adult: 1U/15kg (4–6U) or 1 apheresis | Paeds: 15 mL/kg.

Step 3: Fix Clot Initiation (Clotting Time CT)
  • EXTEM CT > 80 s (with adequate A5_FIB) → 4F-PCC (15–25 IU/kg) OR FFP (10–15 mL/kg).
  • INTEM CT > 240 s & HEPTEM normalizes (CT_IN/CT_HEP ≥ 1.25) → Protamine (25–50 mg).
  • INTEM CT > 240 s & HEPTEM still > 240 s → FFP (10–15 mL/kg).
Step 4: Re-Evaluate

If bleeding continues, repeat ROTEM 10–15 minutes after transfusion completion.
