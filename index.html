3. Guided Transfusion & Medication Plan

Cleaned-Up ROTEM A5 Algorithm

Step 1: Stop Fibrinolysis (Clot Breakdown)

Trigger: A5_EX < 35 mm OR CT_FIB > 600 s OR ML ≥ 5–10%

Action: Tranexamic Acid (TXA) — Trauma: 25 mg/kg IV bolus (<3h) | PPH: 1–2 g IV bolus.

Step 2: Build Clot Firmness (If EXTEM A5 < 35 mm)
Severe Deficit

If A5_EX < 20 mm: Give BOTH Cryo/Fibrinogen + Platelets immediately.

Low Fibrinogen

A5_FIB < 9 mm (Trauma) or < 10.4 mm (PPH).
Target: ≥ 12 mm (Trauma) | ≥ 13.9 mm (PPH).

Low Platelets

A5_FIB ≥ 9 mm (Trauma) or > 10.4 mm (PPH).
Adult: 1U/15kg (4–6U rand) or 1 apheresis | Paeds: 15 mL/kg.

Step 3: Fix Clot Initiation (Clotting Time CT)
  • Extrinsic Factor Deficiency: EXTEM CT > 80 s (after A5_FIB is adequate) → Give 4F-PCC (15–25 IU/kg) OR FFP (10–15 mL/kg).
  • Heparin Effect: INTEM CT > 240 s AND CT_IN / CT_HEP ≥ 1.25 (HEPTEM normalizes) → Give Protamine (25–50 mg).
  • Intrinsic Factor Deficiency: INTEM CT > 240 s AND HEPTEM CT > 240 s → Give FFP (10–15 mL/kg).
Step 4: Re-Evaluate

If bleeding persists after component therapy, draw a new sample 10–15 minutes post-transfusion to repeat ROTEM.
